Privacy Policy

App: Stash Tube
Developer: B4BP Studio
Contact: support@charmastery.com
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, shows no ads and includes no analytics tools.
  • Your browsing and your downloads are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer.
  • The app does not send any data to the developer on its own.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following locally:

  • The history of pages you visit and your bookmarks.
  • Cookies and site data from the websites you visit, as any browser does.
  • Your list of downloads, the downloaded files, and a record of the videos you have already downloaded (so the app can tell you if you find them again).
  • Your settings, including the list of tags you create. The tags you choose for a video are saved with it and inside the downloaded file.
  • Whether you have Premium, as last confirmed by Google Play, so the app works the same when you are offline.

Copied links. When you open the app, it checks whether you have a web link copied, so it can offer to open it. It does this only while the app is on screen, and it first asks the system what kind of content is copied: other copied content, such as passwords or messages, is not read. The link is not sent anywhere; it is only opened if you tap Open. To avoid offering the same link twice, the app keeps a scrambled fingerprint of the last link, not the link itself. You can turn this off in Settings → Offer copied links.

This data is not transmitted to the developer. Incognito tabs do not save history. You can delete this data at any time from Settings (clear browsing data, forget already-downloaded videos) or by uninstalling the app. Downloaded files stay in your device's video folder until you delete them.

Connections the app makes

  • Websites you visit. When you browse or download, the app connects to those sites and their video servers. Like any browser, it sends them your IP address, that site's cookies and technical browser details. How that data is handled depends on each site and its own policy.
  • Home page and search. New tabs open Google's home page. What you type in the search bar is sent to the search engine you have chosen in Settings.
  • Google Play. To show the Premium plans and check whether you have Premium, the app talks to Google Play on your device. See "Purchases" below.
  • Compatibility rule updates. If the developer has set up an update address, the app periodically downloads a file with website compatibility settings from it. That request contains no identifiers and no information about your browsing; the server sees your IP address, as with any download.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play as a yearly subscription or as a one-time purchase. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy and terms. The app receives from Google Play only whether a purchase is active; it does not receive your name, your card or your address, and it does not send purchase information to the developer.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play.

Reporting a site (optional)

If a site doesn't work, you can tap "Report this site". The app prepares a short technical report and opens your phone's share menu, so you decide whether to send it and to whom. Nothing is sent automatically.

The report includes:

  • The address of the page and its domain.
  • The video addresses detected on that page and technical details about its player.
  • The version of the app, of Android and of the browsing component.
  • Your comment, if you write one.

Cookies, credentials and access tokens found in those addresses are removed before the report is prepared.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to tell you about detected videos and show download progress.
  • Foreground service (data sync): to continue downloads you started while the app is in the background.
  • Storage (Android 9 or older only): to save downloaded videos.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Who the data is shared with

The developer does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no other recipients.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Responsible use

Only download content that is yours or that you have permission to download. The app does not download DRM-protected content or content from sites whose terms of use forbid it.

Your rights

Data stored on your device is under your direct control: you can delete it from Settings or by uninstalling the app. For any question about this policy, write to support@charmastery.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with its update date.
